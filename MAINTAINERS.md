## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainership is by invitation from the existing maintainer set. Sustained NVIDIA-internal contribution, demonstrated review judgment on workflow changes, and ProdSec familiarity are the typical criteria. The decision is made by maintainer consensus per [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
